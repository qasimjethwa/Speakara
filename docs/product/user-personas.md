# User personas

Personas are hypotheses built from the product brief and the reference product's public positioning; none has been
validated with user research yet. Validation is task P-01 in the roadmap.

| | **Asha, 19** | **Miguel, 34** | **Claire, 52** | **Mr. Okafor, 41** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Student in Lucknow, Hindi speaker, shared Android phone, patchy data | Delivery driver in Valencia, Spanish speaker, wants English for better jobs | Retired nurse in Leeds, learning Spanish for travel | Secondary-school teacher who wants a free tool for a class of 35 |
| Course | English for Hindi speakers | English for Spanish speakers | Spanish for English speakers | Any |
| Goal | Pass interviews in English | Hold a work conversation | Order food, chat with locals | Homework that tracks itself |
| Blockers today | Runs out of lives after a few mistakes; can't pay | Ads and upsells on breaks; needs speaking practice | Small tap targets; fast audio | No budget; privacy rules |
| What Speakara must do | Never lock her out; work on a low-end phone; light pages | Speaking + role-play free; five-minute sessions | Large targets, slow audio, reduced motion, clear type | Guest mode without accounts; later, class codes |
| Success signal | 7-day streak in week one | 3 tutor conversations a week | Finishes Unit 3 and its story | 60% of class active weekly |

## Design consequences

- 48 px minimum touch targets, slow-audio button and a reduced-motion setting (Claire).
- Guest mode with local persistence and tiny JS-free marketing pages (Asha, Mr. Okafor).
- Skip buttons for speaking/listening so sessions work on a bus (Miguel).
- Native-language instructions are needed for the Hindi and Spanish-speaker courses — now available (interface in Spanish and Hindi; awaiting native review).
