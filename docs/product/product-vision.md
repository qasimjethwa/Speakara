# Product vision

**Speakara makes the complete language-learning experience free.** Not a free tier — the whole thing.

## Why now

The leading app proves that playful, bite-sized practice works at scale. It also gates the parts that matter most to
struggling learners — unlimited mistakes, mistake review, personalised practice, AI conversation — behind subscriptions
(see `docs/research/01-duolingo-product-research.md` §6–7). The learners least able to pay are the ones throttled hardest.

## Principles (each is testable)

| # | Principle | How we hold ourselves to it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Education is accessible to everyone | No learner-facing price anywhere in the product |
| 2 | Nothing stops you learning | No hearts, energy or cooldowns; test: a session can always be started |
| 3 | No paid-only learning features | Every route in `screen-inventory.md` is reachable without payment |
| 4 | AI helps you understand | Tutor prompt forbids writing the learner's next line; corrections carry a reason |
| 5 | Every course has a real curriculum | CEFR-tagged units, guidebooks, content linter, recorded review status |
| 6 | Healthy motivation | Published XP formula, earned-only gems, no loss-framed notifications, bots labelled |
| 7 | Runs on a small budget | Deterministic grading, browser speech, scripted tutor fallback |

## What Speakara is not

Not a chatbot with a streak counter, not a video-course marketplace, not a clone of any other app's brand. Its mascot,
palette, copy and curriculum are original.

## Sustainability

Operating cost is dominated by hosting and optional AI calls. Planned funding, in order of preference: education grants,
individual donations, institutional sponsorship (schools, NGOs, libraries) and paid *services to institutions*
(classroom dashboards, SSO) — never restrictions on learners. Finances are to be published annually.
