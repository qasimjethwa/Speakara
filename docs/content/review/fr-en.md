## fr1 · Greetings — Say hello, be polite and introduce yourself

### Guidebook tips

- **Bonjour first** — In French it is polite to open any exchange with bonjour (or bonsoir in the evening) before asking for anything. _Bonjour, un café, s'il vous plaît._ = Hello, a coffee, please.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Liaison** — A silent final consonant is pronounced when the next word starts with a vowel: vous‿êtes sounds like vou-zêtes, les‿amis like lé-zamis. _Vous êtes Paul ?_ = Are you Paul?  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Tu and vous** — Use tu with friends and children, vous with strangers and to show respect. _Comment vas-tu ?_ = How are you? (informal); _Comment allez-vous ?_ = How are you? (formal)

## fr2 · Au café — Order drinks and food and ask for the bill

### Guidebook tips

- **Gender and articles** — French nouns are masculine (le, un) or feminine (la, une). Learn each noun with its article: le café, la limonade. Before a vowel, le and la become l'. _Le café est chaud._ = The coffee is hot.; _L'eau est fraîche._ = The water is cool.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Je voudrais is polite** — Je voudrais (I would like) is more polite than je veux (I want). Use de l'eau, du pain and du lait for 'some' water, bread and milk. _Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît._ = I would like a coffee, please.; _Je veux de l'eau._ = I want some water.
