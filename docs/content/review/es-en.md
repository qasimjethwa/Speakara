## es1 · Greetings — Say hello, be polite and introduce yourself

### Guidebook tips

- **Two question marks** — Spanish questions open with ¿ and close with ?. Exclamations do the same with ¡ and !. _¿Cómo estás?_ = How are you?; _¡Hola!_ = Hello!  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Greetings change with the clock** — Use buenos días until about noon, buenas tardes until dusk and buenas noches at night — both to greet and to say goodbye. _Buenos días_ = Good morning; _Buenas noches_ = Good night  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **You can drop “yo”** — The verb ending already tells who is speaking, so “Soy Ana” and “Yo soy Ana” both mean “I am Ana”. _Soy Luis._ = I am Luis.; _Me llamo Ana._ = My name is Ana.

## es2 · People and family — Describe yourself, your family and friends

### Guidebook tips

- **Nouns have gender** — Every noun is masculine or feminine. Masculine nouns usually take el / un, feminine nouns take la / una. _el niño, un niño_ = the boy, a boy; _la niña, una niña_ = the girl, a girl

- **Ser: to be** — Use ser for identity and lasting traits: yo soy, tú eres, él / ella es. _Ella es mi amiga._ = She is my friend.; _Tú eres alto._ = You are tall.

## es3 · Food and drink — Order food and say what you eat and drink

### Guidebook tips

- **Comer and beber** — Regular -er verbs share endings: yo como / bebo, tú comes / bebes, él / ella come / bebe. _Yo como pan._ = I eat bread.; _Ella bebe agua._ = She drinks water.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Ordering politely** — “Quiero…, por favor” is clear and friendly in a café. Add con (with) or sin (without) to adjust your order. _Quiero un café con leche, por favor._ = I want a coffee with milk, please.; _Un té sin azúcar._ = A tea without sugar.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **El agua** — Agua is feminine but takes el in the singular because it starts with a stressed a: el agua fría. _El agua, por favor._ = The water, please.
