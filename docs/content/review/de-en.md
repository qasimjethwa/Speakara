## de2 · Im Café — Order drinks and food and ask for things politely

### Guidebook tips

- **The verb is in second place** — In a German main sentence the conjugated verb is always the second idea. Start with another word and the verb still stays second. _Ich trinke Kaffee._ = I drink coffee.; _Heute trinke ich Tee._ = Today I am drinking tea.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Hunger and thirst** — German says ich habe Hunger (I have hunger) and ich habe Durst. Both are nouns, so they are capitalised. _Ich habe Hunger._ = I am hungry.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **der, die, das** — Every noun has a grammatical gender. Learn the article with the noun: der Kaffee (masculine), die Milch (feminine), das Brot (neuter). _Der Kaffee ist heiß._ = The coffee is hot.; _Die Milch ist kalt._ = The milk is cold.
