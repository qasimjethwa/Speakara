## it1 · Saluti — Greet people, say thank you and introduce yourself

### Guidebook tips

- **Two ways to say you** — Use tu with friends and family. Use Lei (capitalised) with strangers and people you respect. Lei takes the verb in the third person. _Come stai?_ = How are you? (informal); _Come sta?_ = How are you? (formal)

## it2 · Al bar — Order coffee and food and ask where things are

### Guidebook tips

- **il, la, un, una** — Italian nouns are masculine or feminine, and the article shows which. Before a vowel, il and la become l'. _Il caffè è buono._ = The coffee is good.; _L'acqua è fresca._ = The water is cool.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Adjectives agree** — Adjectives change with the noun: il formaggio è buono (masculine), la zuppa è deliziosa (feminine). Ho fame (I am hungry, literally I have hunger) never changes. _La zuppa è deliziosa._ = The soup is delicious.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Double consonants matter** — A double consonant can change the meaning. Say it longer: caro (dear) and carro (cart). _Il caro amico._ = The dear friend.
