## ru2 · В кафе — Order drinks and food and ask for the bill

### Guidebook tips

- **Cases change endings** — Russian nouns change their endings with their role. After из (from) the noun takes the genitive case: Москва becomes Москвы. _Я из Москвы._ = I am from Moscow.  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Gender in the past tense** — Past-tense verbs agree with the speaker: я был (said by a man) and я была (said by a woman). _Я был в кафе._ = I was at the café. (said by a man)  
  Reviewer verdict: ☐ correct ☐ needs change — comment:
- **Aspect: платить and заплатить** — Most Russian verbs come in pairs. The imperfective (платить) describes a process or habit; the perfective (заплатить) one completed act. Paying the bill once uses the perfective. _Я хочу́ заплати́ть._ = I want to pay. (now, once); _Я всегда́ плачу́ ка́ртой._ = I always pay by card. (habit)
