## Honest gaps

Speakara is far behind on **content volume**, **native apps**, **audio quality** (browser voices vs. studio recordings) and
**speech scoring** (word overlap vs. phoneme-level). The roadmap orders work to close the content gap first, because a
generous product with one course is still one course.
