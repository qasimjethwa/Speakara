blendshapes, removed viseme shapes and specular maps; converted FBX -> glTF binary with Blender 4.0.2, WebP textures.
Rig: 3ds Max Biped (Bip01_*), fully articulated fingers — identical on both models.
The female model approximates, but is not an exact 3D likeness of, the 2D reference image.
